Research approach

From technological systems to public consequences.

  1. Define the systemIdentify technologies, institutions, actors, infrastructures, and affected communities.
  2. Trace developmentExamine research, finance, production, deployment, adoption, and contestation.
  3. Assess consequencesEvaluate economic, political, security, labor, rights, and distributional effects.
  4. Clarify governanceCompare regulatory options, institutional responsibilities, and implementation conditions.
