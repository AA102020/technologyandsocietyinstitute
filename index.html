Independent research in the public interest

Understanding technological change. Informing public decisions.

The Technology & Society Institute examines how science, technology, institutions, markets, and public values shape one another. We produce rigorous research to clarify the economic, political, security, and social consequences of technological change.

Our mission

Research for governing technology in a changing society.

The Institute focuses on the economics of innovation, technological change, and related regulation. Its mission is to advance knowledge and inform policymakers through independent research, educational programs, policy dialogue, and conferences on major technological and societal questions.
