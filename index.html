Independent research in the public interest

Understanding technological change. Informing public decisions.

The Technology & Society Institute studies how technological and social orders develop together. We examine how institutions and markets shape technological possibilities, and how technologies in turn reorganize economic life, public authority, and social relations.

Our mission

Research on how technological change develops through institutions and reshapes public life.

The Institute advances knowledge and informs public decisions through independent research. Its programs examine how technological systems acquire resources and authority, how organizations put them into practice, and how those arrangements change over time.

Research programs

Researching technology through the institutions that shape its development and use.

Innovation, markets, and economic change

Core perspective

Technology, Society, and Institutions

Technology

Creates possibilities for action while changing how organizations and societies allocate capability and authority.

Society

Shapes what technologies are valued, contested, adopted, and adapted through practical use.

Institutions

Organize the resources and rules through which technologies are developed, governed, and made consequential.
