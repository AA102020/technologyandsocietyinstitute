Research Program 01

Innovation and Economics

Innovation, markets, and economic change

Examines how economic institutions shape technological development and how innovation, in turn, reorganizes markets and productive activity.

Program focus

Innovation is an institutional and economic process.

Scientific discoveries acquire economic value through institutions capable of financing, developing, and diffusing them. The program examines why those arrangements generate different forms of innovation across industries and regions.

Research also asks how technological change alters productivity and competition, and how those changes redistribute economic opportunity.

Areas of inquiry

Core research domains

01

Economics of innovation

How incentives and investment shape the emergence and diffusion of new technologies, and how innovation affects productivity and growth.

02

Firms, markets, and competition

How market structure shapes technological direction and how innovation changes the organization of competition.

03

Finance and financial systems

How financial institutions allocate resources to innovation and how technological change reorganizes financial activity and infrastructure.

04

Labor, skills, and organizations

How organizations adopt technology and how those choices reshape work and the development of economic capability.

05

Innovation systems and economic development

How public institutions and regional systems build technological capability, and how innovation changes patterns of development.

Guiding questions

  • Which institutional arrangements enable technological ideas to become productive capabilities?
  • How do firms and market structure influence the direction and diffusion of innovation?
  • How do financial institutions shape technological development, and how does technological change reshape finance?
  • Under what conditions does technological change increase productivity, and how are its gains distributed?
  • How do technologies reorganize firms and work, and what follows for skills and economic opportunity?
  • Which public institutions build durable technological capabilities across regions and industries?
