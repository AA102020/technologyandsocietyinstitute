Research Program 03

Manufacturing Technologies

Production, infrastructure, and industrial systems

Examines how technologies, skills, standards, supply chains, organizational practices, and public investment convert scientific knowledge and technical design into productive capability.

Program focus

Manufacturing determines whether knowledge becomes durable capability.

Scientific advances and technological designs require production systems, skilled labor, reliable infrastructure, quality control, suppliers, and organizational competence. The program studies the technologies and institutions that translate design into physical capacity.

Research connects advanced manufacturing with productivity, industrial competitiveness, supply-chain resilience, regional development, environmental performance, labor, and national strategy.

Areas of inquiry

Research priorities

01

Advanced manufacturing

Digital production, additive manufacturing, advanced materials, precision systems, and flexible manufacturing.

02

Automation and robotics

Industrial robotics, machine vision, autonomous operations, human-machine collaboration, and workplace redesign.

03

Semiconductors and critical components

Fabrication, equipment, materials, packaging, skills, geographic concentration, and industrial policy.

04

Smart factories

Industrial AI, sensors, digital twins, predictive maintenance, data architecture, and cyber-physical systems.

05

Supply chains and industrial resilience

Supplier networks, critical materials, logistics, inventories, geographic concentration, standards, allied coordination, substitution, and recovery from disruption.

06

Workforce and institutions

Engineering capacity, technical education, apprenticeships, organizational learning, safety, and distributional consequences.

Guiding questions

  • Which production capabilities are strategically difficult to replace?
  • How should policy balance efficiency, resilience, and regional development?
  • How do automation and industrial AI change work, authority, and skill?
  • Which standards and institutions support reliable advanced manufacturing?
