Publications

Research, analysis, and commentary.

The Institute publishes work across its research programs while preserving one canonical record for each publication and its relationship to the broader research agenda.

Publication architecture

Four publication series support different stages and forms of research.

The publication system separates substantial completed research from work circulated for further development, concise research synthesis, and shorter analytical interpretation. Each publication receives a stable TSI code within its series.

Publication series organize outputs. Research programs organize the questions the Institute studies. A publication may connect to more than one program while retaining one canonical record.

Publication series

Institute publications

01

Research Papers

TSI-RP-YYYY-##

Substantial original research presenting evidence, analysis, and developed conclusions.

02

Working Papers

TSI-WP-YYYY-##

Research circulated while analysis remains open to revision, extension, empirical testing, or scholarly comment.

03

Research Briefs

TSI-RB-YYYY-##

Concise research outputs focused on a bounded problem, finding, institutional development, or policy-relevant implication.

04

Commentary

TSI-CM-YYYY-##

Shorter analytical essays interpreting consequential technological or institutional developments.

Publication records

Stable records, visible revision.

Each formal publication will have one canonical record containing its title, author, series, date, research relationship, TSI code, and version information where applicable.

Material revisions will remain identifiable rather than silently replacing the intellectual history of released work. Publications will be added here as their records are established.
