Research Program 04

Military Technologies

Military technology, institutions, and security

Examines how military organizations turn technical systems into operational capability and how new technologies, in turn, reshape military organization and strategy.

Program focus

Military technology acquires value through institutions and force employment.

Technical superiority becomes military capability through organizational adoption and operational practice. The program examines how military institutions integrate new systems, adapt around emerging capabilities, and learn from operational experience.

Research also examines how acquisition choices shape technological development and how fielded capabilities alter strategic relationships and demands for institutional restraint.

Areas of inquiry

Core research domains

01

Military Systems and Operational Integration

Examines how military organizations integrate new technologies into operational practice and how experience under contested conditions reshapes technological development and use.

02

Acquisition, Industry, and Capability Formation

Examines how military institutions translate technological possibilities into fielded capability and how acquisition choices reshape industrial capacity and subsequent technological development.

03

Force Adaptation and Military Effectiveness

Examines how military organizations adapt around new capabilities and under what conditions those adaptations translate technological advantage into military effectiveness.

04

Strategy, Governance, and Technological Change

Examines how changing military capabilities reshape strategic relationships and how institutions establish responsibility and restraint around their development and use.

Guiding questions

  • Under what conditions do new technologies improve military effectiveness?
  • How do military organizations integrate new capabilities and adapt operational practice around them?
  • How do acquisition institutions shape which technologies are developed, produced, and fielded?
  • How do technological changes alter strategic relationships and patterns of escalation?
  • What institutional arrangements preserve responsibility and restraint as military capabilities change?
